Listen to live Internet radio with OpenRadio, a native Android radio player.

Features:
- Search radio stations by name, country, and genre.
- Play live audio streams and manage favorite stations.
- Add custom stations by URL.
- Continue listening in the background with Media3 and media notifications.
- Use a sleep timer and audio equalizer controls.
- Customize the interface with themes and display settings.

Station availability and stream conditions depend on external providers. An Internet connection is needed to play live radio. Some stations use HTTP connections.

Source code: https://github.com/latanvillegas/Radio-Escucha
